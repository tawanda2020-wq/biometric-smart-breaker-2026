Operator Registration

Digits only (0-9), exactly 4 - the physical keypad has no letters the operator could type this with other than A-D, so a numeric PIN is the only thing guaranteed to be enterable. Stored as a SHA-256 hash, never in plain text.

After registering, the operator places their finger on the sensor when the device prompts — the microcontroller checks for new registrations automatically and walks them through enrolment.

What do these events mean?
ONA verified operator scanned their finger, entered their PIN, and confirmed in time — the breaker energised.
OFFThe breaker was switched off, either by the panel button or the MCB's own automatic trip.
DENIEDA login attempt failed — wrong fingerprint or wrong PIN. The breaker stayed off.
TIMEOUTA correct login wasn't confirmed with a second key press in time, so it expired safely.
LOCKOUTToo many failed attempts in a row — the device paused all logins for 30 seconds.
